Delete it yourself (instant)

  1. Open your chat with the AgriConnect bot — on Facebook Messenger (AgriConnect M'lang page), by SMS, or in the chat on our website.
  2. Send: DELETE MY DATA
  3. The bot asks you to confirm. Reply: CONFIRM DELETE

Your data is deleted immediately and the bot confirms it.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, Messenger ID, and website-chat ID (your profile becomes "Deleted user").
  • All messages between you and the bot, and your entries in the staff inbox.
  • Your notifications and any unfinished registration.
  • The original text of your requests.

Past orders remain in suppliers' records without your name or contact details, so suppliers can keep accurate sales records.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't use the chat, send a message to the AgriConnect M'lang Facebook Page saying you want your data deleted. Staff will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
