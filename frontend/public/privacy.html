Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

AgriConnect connects small-scale farmers in M'lang, Cotabato with verified agri-input suppliers through a website, SMS, Facebook Messenger, and a website chat. It is operated as a student capstone project. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Data we collect

  • Profile: your name, barangay, municipality, and (optionally) your mobile number.
  • Channel identifiers: your Facebook Messenger page-scoped ID when you message our Facebook Page, or a random ID stored in your browser when you use the website chat.
  • Messages: the text you send to the AgriConnect bot and the bot's replies.
  • Orders: the products, quantities, delivery place and dates you request, supplier quotations, orders, deliveries, and ratings you give.
  • Suppliers: store name, contact person, phone number, location, delivery areas, and product prices.

We do not collect passwords, payment card details, your Facebook friends, or your location from your device.

2. How we use it

  • To register you and let your barangay, LGU, or cooperative verify your profile.
  • To read your messages (automatically, with our language-processing and machine-learning software) and turn them into farm-input requests.
  • To send your requests to verified suppliers and send you their quotations, order updates, and replies.
  • To group requests from the same barangay so suppliers can offer lower prices and fewer trips.
  • To produce anonymous statistics (for example, number of requests and trips avoided).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Verified suppliers see your requests (product, quantity, barangay, needed date) and, once you accept a quotation, the details needed to deliver your order.
  • Barangay, LGU, or cooperative staff see profiles and messages to verify farmers and help with orders and complaints.
  • Service providers: Render (web hosting), Meta Platforms (delivers Facebook Messenger messages), and, if enabled, an SMS gateway. They process data only to provide their service.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use AgriConnect. You can delete it at any time (section 5). This prototype's server may also reset its data when it is updated.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To delete it yourself, send DELETE MY DATA to the AgriConnect bot on Messenger, SMS, or the website chat, then reply CONFIRM DELETE. Full instructions: Data deletion.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS. Messages from Facebook are checked with Meta's signature before they are accepted. Access keys are stored only on the server, never in the app's code.

7. Children

AgriConnect is intended for farmers and suppliers aged 18 and over.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, send a message to the AgriConnect M'lang Facebook Page, or type staff in the chat to reach the staff.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
